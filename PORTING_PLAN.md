## 6. On-console log standard

The game log lives at `<DATA_PATH>logs/<slug>_NNN.log` (`<slug>` = raymanjunglerun), incrementing
001 to 999 with `next.idx` (never `log_<timestamp>.txt`, never 000-based, never a hardcoded
path: use `DATA_PATH`). The FTP upload creates `logs/` and `saves/` on the console even when
empty. Toolkit commands (no AI, deterministic): `psvita-toolkit log-standard --fix-dirs` (audits
and creates the dirs), `psvita-toolkit log-trace --ensure-flag` (injects per-function
`[TRACE] >> f()` traces under `#ifdef PORT_TRACE`; build with `-DPORT_TRACE=ON` for debug,
`OFF` for production, or `psvita-toolkit log-trace --remove` to strip them from the tree).
